# BugFlow Elite — Phase 6 Disclosure and Research Priority

## Objective

Convert verified findings into high-quality, human-reviewable disclosure intents while keeping submission authority outside the scanner.

## Evidence pack

A report intent carries explicit evidence IDs, reproduction steps, references, and attachment hashes. A READY_TO_SUBMIT state requires a high evidence-pack score.

## Human approval

The disclosure gate fails closed unless the finding is in scope, verified, non-duplicate, sufficiently evidenced, and explicitly human-approved. The project does not auto-submit bounty reports.

## Prioritization

Research priority combines severity signal, confidence, novelty, change signal, and threat intelligence. Priority is a queueing signal and never overrides severity or disclosure policy.
