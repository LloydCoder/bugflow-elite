# BugFlow Elite — Phase 11 Ecosystem Integration

## Objective

Connect BugFlow to the wider Tinlance intelligence and engineering ecosystem without duplicating authority.

## Integration map

- **TADS → BugFlow:** account and demand signals can prioritize research.
- **ReconOS → BugFlow:** assets, observations, and evidence references enrich the attack-surface graph.
- **BugFlow → FDSE:** verified findings and evidence packs can become engineering tasks.
- **BugFlow → FDSE Toolkit:** field-ready security evidence and delivery artifacts can be packaged for engagements.
- **World Intelligence → BugFlow:** entity, event, and change signals can become research context.

All integration contracts are advisory/data-plane contracts. Execution authority remains governed by the Tinlance Agent Platform boundary; BugFlow never assumes external signals authorize an action.

## Commercial surfaces

The contracts support three operating modes without changing the research core: managed BugFlow SaaS, MSSP/partner delivery, and embedded enterprise research services. Compliance and enterprise reporting should consume the same evidence and audit artifacts rather than creating parallel truth systems.
